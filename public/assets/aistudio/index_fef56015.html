About the channel

Cinematic stillness, carried by wind and rotor blades.

Heino Xide is a one-person film practice built around a Mavic Air 2 and a quiet respect for the land it flies over — the coastlines of Kyaukphyu, the hills of Rakhine, and the small human moments in between.

Each video is graded slowly, scored gently, and released without hurry. The channel isn't chasing scale — it's keeping a record of places worth remembering exactly as they felt.
